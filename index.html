How this works

This tool is preloaded with Store 758's real staff roster, availability, and store hours — the same data as the Excel workbook. It also saves to a shared cloud backend, so whatever you generate here shows up the same way on any other computer that opens this page.

Quick start

  1. Staff Roster — add, edit, or remove employees. Each person has a job title, a weekly target/max, a minimum hours floor, an optional Hours/Day, and a day-by-day availability pattern.
  2. Store Hours — the store's open/close time for each day of the week. Shifts are never generated outside these hours. Click Apply Store Hours to confirm any changes.
  3. Time-Off Requests — log a request for a specific employee and week; those days are automatically skipped when you generate that week's schedule.
  4. Schedule — pick or add a week, set its start date (click Apply Date to confirm), enter a Total Hours Budget for that week (click Apply Budget to confirm — required before Generate will run), then click Generate Week to auto-draft shifts. Click any day cell to fine-tune it by hand afterward. Manually edited cells are marked with a small ✎ and are never overwritten by Generate.
  5. Week History — every week you've ever clicked Generate on, kept permanently in the cloud so it's the same list no matter which computer you open this on. Click any row to view that week read-only.

Every change anywhere in this app needs a button click to confirm it (Apply Changes, Apply Store Hours, Apply Date, Apply Budget, or a shift's Save button) — nothing applies just from typing or clicking away. Once confirmed, it's saved automatically to this browser; no separate save step.

Total Hours Budget

Each week has its own Total Hours Budget — the combined hours for the entire staff that week (e.g. 130). Generate always respects: each employee's own availability, store hours, time-off requests, and their individual Min/Max Hrs — the weekly budget is an additional ceiling on top of all of that, not a replacement for it.

When the budget isn't enough to cover everyone at their normal individual target, anyone with an Hours/Day value set is filled first — they get scheduled toward their own normal target before anyone else, in roster order. Whatever's left of the budget is then spread across everyone else, also in roster order. If the budget runs out, people further down the list may get zero hours that week — that's expected, not a bug.

The banner above the schedule grid always shows the running total vs. the budget, and turns red if it's ever over — including from manual edits, which are allowed but flagged rather than blocked.

Two more scheduling rules, always on

  • Employees with an Hours/Day value work exactly that many hours on every day they're scheduled. Set it per person in the Staff Roster — e.g. 8 for a full-timer, 4 for someone who always works a short shift. Leave it blank for anyone whose shift length should flex to fill their target instead (the old behavior). A day is only ever shortened below that value if the real window that day is smaller (e.g. Sunday's shorter store hours) or the shared Total Hours Budget runs out — it's never stretched longer. If someone's Hours/Day × available days would push them over their own Weekly Target/Max Hrs, Generate schedules them fewer days at the full Hours/Day amount rather than shortening their shifts. Anyone whose availability already limits them to 5 or fewer days just works all of those at their Hours/Day rate (this is how Mohamad and the managers get their pattern — see below). Anyone fully available gets 2 days off chosen for them: managers rotate their 2 days off week to week (see Manager coverage below), and everyone else with an Hours/Day value defaults to Saturday+Sunday off — currently that's just Tatiana, giving her Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM every week.
  • Nobody on staff works more than 5 days a week. Everyone gets at least 2 days off, every week — Hours/Day employees per the rule above, and everyone else by capping Generate to their 5 longest-window available days instead of spreading thin across every day they're available. Part-time Sales Advisors never get a shift shorter than 3.5 hours — if the waterfilled share for a day would land under that, Generate either rounds it up to 3.5 (when the day's window and remaining budget allow) or drops that day for them entirely rather than leaving a too-short shift on the schedule.

Both rules only govern what Generate does automatically. Manual edits can still override them on individual cells if you need an exception — those cells just won't be touched by Generate afterward.

Full-timer fixed shift menu (Joshua, Mohamad, Tatiana, Andrea, Jasmine Davis)

The 5 employees with Hours/Day set to 8 aren't started at just any time — Generate only ever picks from a specific menu of start times, and every one of those shifts runs 9 hours start-to-end with a 1-hour unpaid lunch break built in, so it's 8 paid hours inside a 9-hour shift:

  • Sun: 8:00 AM–5:00 PM or 10:00 AM–7:00 PM only.
  • Mon–Thu: 8:00–5:00, 9:00–6:00, 10:00–7:00, 11:00–8:00, or 12:00–9:00.
  • Fri–Sat: all of the Mon–Thu options, plus 1:00 PM–10:00 PM.

Generate picks the earliest of these that fits the person's actual availability that day. Day cells show the break right in the label, e.g. "8:00 AM–5:00 PM (8h +1h break)". Manually editing one of these cells shows an Unpaid Break field too, so you can add or adjust a break on a manual edit the same way. Mohamad's cells also pick the latest-fitting option on his designated closing days — see below.

Manager coverage — always a manager on, always a manager closing

Managers (Joshua, Andrea, Jasmine Davis) don't just pick any shift menu time independently — Generate coordinates the three of them so the store always has manager coverage: Sunday through Friday, exactly 2 managers are on (one opening, one closing), and Saturday has all 3 (opener, mid-shift, closer). Managers can and do overlap — that's intentional, not a mistake. Each manager's cell shows their role that day ("Opening" / "Mid-shift" / "Closing") right under the time.

Since each manager is capped at 40 hrs/8-hr days = 5 days/week, this works out to everyone working every Saturday, plus 4 of the other 6 days — so each manager gets exactly 2 days off, always on a Sun–Fri day, never two managers off on the same day, and never on Saturday. Whose 2 days off fall where rotates by week so it's not always the same person missing the same days.

Who closes each night follows a deliberate split rather than falling to whoever happens to be scheduled first: Jasmine Davis (Store Manager) closes exactly 2 nights a week, and Joshua and Andrea (Department Managers) split the week's other closing nights between them, balanced night-to-night so it alternates rather than one of them getting stuck with most of the closes. The one rule that always wins over that balance: nobody closes more than 2 nights in a row — if the usual pick would make it a 3rd consecutive closing night for someone, the close goes to whichever other manager is on shift that day instead.

The Coverage Summary at the bottom of the Schedule tab has a Manager Coverage row for every day — it shows "✓ Full" plus who's closing, or flags "⚠ Gap" / "✗ None" if staffing, time-off, or a manual edit ever breaks the guarantee, so a problem is always visible rather than silent.

Mohamad's closing shifts

As the FT Sales Advisor, Mohamad can work any shift on the menu, but always gets up to 3 closing shifts spread across his week (never back-to-back) rather than being clustered together — his cells show "Closing" the same way a manager's do on those days.

Color-coded roles & list order

The Staff Roster and Schedule tables both show a colored Role badge per person — Manager Visual Full-Time Staff — plus a matching colored stripe on the Name cell. Both tables (and the CSV export, and Week History detail view) list Managers first, then Visuals, then other full-timers, then everyone else, so the people running the store are always at the top of the list.

Saving your work & using it on another computer

Every change you make (editing an employee, generating a week, adding time-off, etc.) saves itself automatically to this browser on this computer — there's no extra "save" step. It also sends that same save up to the cloud in the background; watch the little ☁ status text next to the "Saved on this device" note, top right, to see whether that last sync went through.

Because of that cloud save, opening this page on a different computer or phone will automatically load the latest version — whatever was last saved from any device — the moment the page loads or you refresh it. It's not instant/live while you're both looking at it at the same time, just current as of your last refresh. If the cloud can't be reached for some reason, the app just falls back to the last version saved on that device, so it never breaks — it'll simply say "☁ Offline" instead of "☁ Synced."

⬇ Download Backup / 📂 Restore Backup still work exactly as before, for an extra off-device copy or moving data around by hand if you ever want to.

Use 🖨️ Print / Save PDF on the Schedule tab to print the current week or save it as a PDF for posting or sharing. Use Export CSV (on the Schedule tab) to download the current week as a spreadsheet file you can open in Excel.

Notes

  • The Status column flags Under if someone is below their weekly minimum, and Over if they're above their weekly target — both are just flags to review, nothing is blocked.
